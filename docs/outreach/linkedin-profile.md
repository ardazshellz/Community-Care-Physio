# About

I’m Zakery Shelley, a UK physiotherapist and CSP member, registered with the HCPC under PH132358. I run Community Care Physio, providing home visits across South West London, including Wimbledon, Putney, Wandsworth, Kingston and Richmond. I have an NHS community rehabilitation background.

I work with case managers, personal injury rehabilitation coordinators and home care agency managers. My clinical work covers neurological rehabilitation, falls and frailty, recovery after hospital discharge and post-surgical recovery.

I offer an initial assessment at home within ten working days of the go ahead and provide a written report within five working days of assessment. I select appropriate baseline measures from Timed Up and Go, Tinetti, Berg, Elderly Mobility Scale, Edmonton Frail Scale, 6CIT and Goal Attainment Scaling. I record dated outcome scores at initial assessment, review and discharge.

I begin treatment only once funding is agreed. I provide progress and discharge reports, keep all liaison in writing and supply password protected PDF reports.

I work in line with the Rehabilitation Code 2015 as a treating therapist only. I do not undertake expert witness work. I hold enhanced DBS clearance, indemnity insurance and public liability insurance.
