# Outreach — home care agencies (Lane 1)

Draft only, 5 Oct 2026. Nothing sent. Targets, phones and managers are in `pipeline-targets.md` (Lane 1, 12 agencies). Send from infoccphysio@gmail.com via the owner's Chrome. Gmail inserts the signature, so type the body above "Kind regards". Style rules applied: no dashes or hyphens between words, no semicolons, no mention of the NHS post.

No attachment. The referral pack is written for case managers and funded work. Agency clients pay privately, so the email points to the website instead.

Order of work: email or form first, then the call at 4:15 pm two working days later. Only Caremark Merton and Visiting Angels publish an email address. The other ten use a website form, so use version D2 there.

Hello [name],

My name is Zakery Shelley. I am an HCPC registered physiotherapist (PH132358) and I run Community Care Physio, a home visit service across South West London, including [area].

I am writing because your carers are often the first to notice when a client is slowing down, has had a fall or has come home from hospital weaker than before. When a family asks you who could help, I would like to be a name you can give them.

What I offer your clients:

1. A falls and mobility assessment at home within ten working days of the enquiry.
2. A written summary the family can share with the GP.
3. A simple home programme, with the regular carer shown how to support it safely during normal visits if the family wishes.

My background is NHS community rehabilitation. Most of my work is with older adults, with falls and frailty, neurological conditions such as stroke and Parkinson's, and recovery after hospital or surgery. Each client keeps the same physiotherapist from the first visit to the last. Visits are in the late afternoon, in the evening and at weekends, when relatives can usually be there.

There is no cost or commitment for the agency. Families book and pay directly, and prices are on the website. I am fully insured and hold an enhanced DBS, and I can send certificates if you would like them on file.

If it would help, I can send a one page summary for your office and care team.

Kind regards,

## D3. Call script, 4:15 pm

Aim of the call: a named person and permission to send one page. Not a sale. Two minutes at most.

**Reception**

"Good afternoon. My name is Zakery Shelley, I am a local physiotherapist. Could I speak to [manager name], or whoever looks after client care? It will take two minutes."

If asked what it is about: "I do home visit physiotherapy in [area]. I sent a short note on [day] and wanted to put a voice to it."

If the manager is not available: "No problem. What is the best email address to reach [him or her] directly? And is there a better time to call?" Write both down. Do not pitch to reception.

**Manager**

"Thank you for taking the call, I will be brief. I am an HCPC registered physiotherapist and I visit people at home across [area]. I am calling because your carers will often be the first to see a client who is getting unsteady or has come home from hospital weaker. When a family asks you who could help, I would like to be someone you can mention."

Pause. Then one question:

"Do families ask you about physiotherapy at the moment? Who do you point them to?"

Listen. Then, to fit the answer:

- **They have nobody:** "Then I may be useful. I assess at home within ten working days, leave the family a written summary for the GP, and show the regular carer the exercises if the family wants that. Families pay me directly, so there is nothing for you to arrange."
- **They already use someone:** "That is good to hear. I would be glad to be a second name for when they are full or the client is in [area]. I keep a small caseload, so the client always sees me."
- **They send people to the GP or NHS:** "That is the right first step. I am the option for families who do not want to wait, or who want more visits than the NHS service can give."

**Close**

"Could I email you one page you can keep in the office? What is the best address?" Read the address back.

If the manager is warm: "Would it be useful if I dropped in for ten minutes one afternoon after four to meet the team?"

"Thank you for your time."

**Voicemail**

"Hello, this is a message for [manager name]. My name is Zakery Shelley, a home visit physiotherapist covering [area]. I help older clients after falls or a hospital stay, and I would like to be a name you can give families who ask. My number is 07508 401627, and I will send a short email as well. Thank you."

**Common questions**

- **Cost:** "Prices are on the website. Families pay me directly and travel is included across the SW postcodes."
- **Commission or referral fee:** "I do not pay or take referral fees. It keeps the recommendation clean for both of us."
- **Insurance and checks:** "HCPC registered, professional indemnity and public liability insurance, enhanced DBS. I can email the certificates today."
- **Will you train our carers:** "I show the regular carer the programme for that one client, with the family's consent. I do not run moving and handling training."
- **Daytime visits:** "Late afternoon, evenings and weekends. Most families prefer that because someone can be there."
