# Goal Attainment Scaling (GAS) — worksheet and how-to

Use at the initial assessment. Two or three goals per client is plenty. Score at each progress report and at discharge.

## How to write one goal (about 5 minutes)

1. **Ask the client** (or family) what they most want to do again. Use their words: "walk to the corner shop", "sleep in my own bed upstairs", "get in and out of the car".
2. **Make it measurable**: distance, time, number of repetitions, level of help, aid used.
3. **Set the expected level (0)**: what you honestly predict after the agreed number of sessions. Not the best case.
4. **Fill the other four levels** around it. Each step should be a clear, observable change.
5. **Baseline today is usually −1 or −2.** Write which.

## Scoring at review

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| −2 | Much less than expected (often = baseline, or worse) |
| −1 | Somewhat less than expected |
| 0 | Expected outcome achieved |
| +1 | Somewhat better than expected |
| +2 | Much better than expected |

Report each goal's score and one line of evidence ("walked 250 m with stick, one rest, 2 Oct"). Case managers don't need the T-score maths; a table of goals and scores is enough.

## Worked example

**Goal 1 — walk to the corner shop (250 m) with daughter**
Baseline: 30 m indoors with stick, no outdoor walking (−2)

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| −2 | Indoors only, under 50 m |
| −1 | Outdoors to the gate and back (about 60 m) with stick and supervision |
| 0 | 250 m to the shop with stick, one seated rest, daughter alongside |
| +1 | 250 m with stick, no rest |
| +2 | 250 m alone with stick |

**Goal 2 — sleep in own bed upstairs**
Baseline: sleeping downstairs, cannot attempt stairs (−2)

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| −2 | Not attempting stairs |
| −1 | 4 steps up and down with rail and supervision |
| 0 | Full flight up and down once a day with rail, supervised |
| +1 | Full flight independently with rail, once a day |
| +2 | Stairs independently as needed through the day |

## Tips

- If a client scores +2 early, the 0 level was set too low; say so in the report and re-set.
- Keep levels about function, not impairment ("walks to the shop", not "Berg 45").
- One goal can be about carer confidence ("daughter transfers Mum to the car without help").
