How it works

  1. Book online or by phone. Pick a date and time that suits you; evenings and weekends are available.
  2. We come to you. Your physiotherapist assesses you in your own home, where you actually move and live.
  3. A plan you can follow. Hands-on treatment, exercises and clear next steps, with follow-ups booked around you.

Who we see

Most of the people we see are older adults, people recovering after a hospital stay or operation, and people living with neurological conditions. Family members, carers and case managers can book on someone’s behalf. Refer a patient.
