Four steps, clear timescales

  1. ReferUse the online form or email the referral details. We acknowledge within one working day and confirm whether we can accept the referral. No clinical documents by open email; a secure transfer is arranged once accepted.
  2. AssessA 60-minute initial assessment at home within ten working days of your go-ahead: history, mobility, balance, strength, transfers, home environment and the client's own goals, with standard outcome measures (Timed Up and Go, Tinetti and others as indicated).
  3. ReportAssessment report within five working days of the visit: findings, baseline measures, SMART goals, recommended number and frequency of sessions, equipment needs and a costed plan. Treatment starts only once you confirm funding.
  4. Treat and reviewVisits at the agreed frequency. Progress reports at the intervals you set, typically every six sessions or three months, and a discharge report with community and equipment recommendations when goals are met.
