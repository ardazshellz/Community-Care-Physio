# Coverage map data attribution

The geographical paths in coverage-districts.json are adapted from © Wikipedia contributors' postcode district boundaries, via https://github.com/missinglink/uk-postcode-polygons . The source dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . The adapted geographical data is distributed under the same licence.

Changes: selected South West London and neighbouring districts; projected coordinates for SVG rendering; calculated interior label positions; rounded projected coordinates to one decimal place. Downloaded 15 September 2026. These are approximate postcode boundaries, not council borough boundaries. The postcode entered at booking determines coverage.

The user's reference map was used for visual direction only; its image and artwork are not redistributed.

River Thames polygon: Greater London Authority, webmap_context_layer MapServer layer 1 (https://gis.london.gov.uk/arcgis/rest/services/apps/webmap_context_layer/MapServer/1), retrieved 15 September 2026 in WGS84. Clipped and projected to the same map coordinates as the district polygons. River geography is a separate source from the CC BY-SA postcode boundaries.
