Home visit physiotherapy
for the people you care for

When a client is slowing down, has had a fall or has come home from hospital weaker, families often ask their care team who can help. This page is for your office to keep and share.

When to think of us

  • A fall, a near miss, or new unsteadiness
  • Home from hospital and not back to their usual level
  • Struggling with stairs, transfers, or getting out of a chair
  • Stroke, Parkinson's, MS or another neurological condition
  • Recovering after a hip, knee or other operation
  • Walking less, going out less, losing confidence

What the family receives

  • A falls and mobility assessment at home, within ten working days of the enquiry
  • A written summary they can share with the GP
  • A simple home programme, with the regular carer shown how to support it safely, if the family wishes
  • The same physiotherapist at every visit
  • Visits in the late afternoon, evening and at weekends, when relatives can be there

How it works

  1. The family calls, messages or books online. No GP referral is needed.
  2. We agree a time and confirm the price before the visit.
  3. First visit: a 60 minute assessment at home, with treatment started the same day.
  4. Follow-up visits only if they are needed and wanted.
