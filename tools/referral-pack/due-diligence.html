Clinical governance

  • Standards: HCPC standards of proficiency, conduct, performance and ethics, and the CSP code of professional values. Safeguarding, basic life support and manual handling training kept current
  • Records: contemporaneous notes, kept 8 years, stored encrypted, reports sent password protected
  • Outcome measures: TUG and Tinetti at baseline, Berg Balance Scale, Elderly Mobility Scale, Edmonton Frail Scale and 6CIT where indicated, repeated at review and discharge
  • Safety: NEWS2 observations at the first three sessions and when indicated, written GP liaison when findings need medical review, escalation to 111 or 999
  • Consent and capacity: written consent at the first visit, Mental Capacity Act principles applied

How we work with you

  • Referral acknowledged within one working day
  • Initial assessment at home within ten working days of your go ahead
  • Written assessment report within five working days of the visit, with a costed plan, then progress and discharge reports
  • Fees, timescales and a sample report are in our referral pack; contractual terms are in our terms of engagement
  • Invoices numbered and itemised, 30 day terms, no fee for GP liaison

Safeguarding and complaints

Adults at risk are safeguarded under the Care Act 2014 and local procedures, with concerns raised with the case manager and the local safeguarding team the same day. Complaints are acknowledged within two working days and answered in writing within twenty, with the HCPC and ICO as independent routes. Lone working uses a check in and check out routine for every visit.
