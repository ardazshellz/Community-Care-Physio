4Clinical governance

  • Standards. Practice follows the HCPC Standards of Proficiency for Physiotherapists and the HCPC Standards of Conduct, Performance and Ethics, and the CSP Code of Members' Professional Values and Behaviour.
  • Assessment. Full subjective and objective assessment at the first visit, including home environment, falls history, medication review and goals. Outcome measures: Timed Up and Go and Tinetti at baseline, with the Berg Balance Scale, Elderly Mobility Scale, Edmonton Frail Scale and 6CIT where indicated, repeated at review and discharge.
  • Physiological safety. NEWS2 observations recorded at each of the first three sessions and whenever clinically indicated. Treatment is deferred and the GP informed where the client is not medically fit on the day. Written liaison with the GP where findings need medical review.
  • Records. Contemporaneous clinical notes for every contact, stored encrypted, retained for 8 years after discharge. Reports sent as password protected PDFs.
  • Consent and capacity. Written consent at the first visit, before assessment and before any information is shared. Mental Capacity Act 2005 principles applied, with best interests decisions involving family and the case manager where capacity is lacking.
  • Incidents. Recorded at the time and reported to the instructing organisation within one working day.

5Safeguarding and lone working

  • Adults at risk are safeguarded in line with the Care Act 2014 and the procedures of the relevant local authority safeguarding adults board. Concerns are raised with the instructing case manager and the local safeguarding team the same working day.
  • Lone working follows a check in and check out routine for every home visit, with a nominated contact aware of each visit.
  • Safeguarding adults policy and lone working procedure available on request.

6Information governance

  • Personal and special category data are processed under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, under Article 9(2)(h) for the provision of health care.
  • Records are held in an encrypted booking and records system with nightly encrypted backups. Card payments are processed by Stripe; card details are never held by the practice.
  • Clinical documents are sent as password protected PDFs with the password agreed at acceptance and sent separately. Privacy notice published at communitycarephysio.co.uk.

9Invoicing and cancellation

  • Invoices are numbered (CCP-YYYY-NNNN), itemised by visit date and quote your case reference and purchase order number.
  • Issued monthly in arrears or with each report. Payment by BACS within 30 days. Bank details appear on every invoice.
  • Treatment may be paused where an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, with notice to you and the client.
  • Visits cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or where the client is not available, are charged at the session fee. No charge where the cancellation is ours.
  • The written quote and our terms of engagement (v1, October 2026) govern each instruction.

10Complaints

  • Complaints are acknowledged within 2 working days and answered in writing within 20 working days.
  • Independent routes: the Health and Care Professions Council for professional conduct and the Information Commissioner's Office for data handling.
